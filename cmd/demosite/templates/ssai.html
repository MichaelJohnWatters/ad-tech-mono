Live channel · Server-Side Ad Insertion (SSAI)

Unlike a YouTube-style pre-roll (where the player fetches a VAST tag and inserts the ad itself), a live/CTV stream uses SSAI: the ad breaks are stitched into the manifest server-side. The player just plays one continuous stream — it can't tell ad from content, can't skip, can't block. As the player walks the manifest and fetches each ad segment, the server (not the browser) fires the impression + quartile beacons. Each break is a real SSP→Exchange→DSP auction on its own billable trace.
