  1. Deploy the demo world: global.demosites=true → make deploy-demosites (publisher sites) + make deploy-demostore (the shop).

{{ template "card-end" . }} {{ template "card-start" (dict "title" "💎 First-party audience demo — upload a segment, watch an advertiser bid it up" "subtitle" "Onboard a high-value audience (diamond & jewelry intenders + affluent) and watch Lumière Diamonds bid a DOMINANT CPM to win that visitor's display + video — the premium shows up as a high clearing price in the trace panel.") }}

Two ways to run it. A is pre-seeded and always works (great for a recording); B is the live "I'll upload myself right now" moment. Both feed the same segment, so the advertiser bids the same way.

A · Seeded persona (reliable — nothing to upload)
  1. Open any demo publisher site (e.g. chronicle.adtech.local for display, twitchr.adtech.local for video/CTV).
  2. On the cookie banner click “Accept personalized” (the hashed email only rides the request under personalization consent).
  3. The default persona (demo.shopper@example.com) is pre-enrolled in Diamond & Jewelry Intenders + Affluent. Watch the 🔍 Trace panel: the ad request shows ✓ FILL lumiere-diamonds.com at a high price (≈ $70–90 CPM vs the ~$10 open market), and the video/CTV spot is the branded Lumière Diamonds card.
B · Live CRM upload ("upload myself")
  1. Pick your email and compute its hash (the SDK sends sha256(lowercased email)):
    printf '%s' you@example.com | tr 'A-Z' 'a-z' | shasum -a 256
  2. Act as Lumière Diamonds (View as account → Lumière Diamonds) and upload the hash into the targeted segment — name it exactly Diamond & Jewelry Intenders, visibility public, so it merges into the segment the campaign already targets: 
    curl -sk https://gateway.adtech.local/v1/api/audiences \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer <lumiere-session-jwt>" \
  -H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
  -d '{"name":"Diamond & Jewelry Intenders","visibility":"public",
       "user_ids":["<your-sha256-hash>"]}'
    (or the advertiser portal's Audiences → New upload with a CSV whose hashed_email column holds your hash).
  3. On a demo site, set your persona to that same email (persona bar bottom-left → Change), accept personalized consent, and reload. Same result as A — Lumière Diamonds wins your impressions.

How it works: the SSP matches segments on the hashed_email the SDK forwards (consent-gated); these two segments are public, so the SSP stamps them on user.ext.segments, our DSP reads them for targeting, and profiles/dsps/luxe.yaml applies a big per-segment bid modifier on a high base bid → the dominant, first-price clearing price you see. After a reset: make demo-data re-seeds the segments + persona; make deploy-demosites for the branded video.

{{ template "card-end" . }} {{ template "card-start" (dict "title" "🛍️ The earned-audience money loop — step by step" "subtitle" "Browse the shop → get chased by a Dynamic Product Ad on a publisher site → purchase converts. This is THE end-to-end demo; it's order- and consent-sensitive, so follow it exactly.") }}
  1. Open the shop → shop.adtech.local. On the cookie banner click “Accept personalized” (NOT “Contextual only” — contextual drops the id cookie and nothing gets enrolled).
  2. Click a product — open the product page (the F‑150 / kibble item), not just the home page. That fires the /v1/t/rt pixel and enrolls you into “Shop Product Viewers”. Watch it in the 🔍 Trace panel: an RT “retargeting pixel” row.
  3. Wait ~3 seconds. Enrollment is real-time but not instant (pixel → audience‑rt → Redis ≈ 3s).
  4. Open a publisher site in the SAME browser → e.g. chronicle.adtech.local. Click “Accept personalized” here too. Don’t click “New visitor”/“Change” on either persona bar — both sites default to demo.shopper@example.com, which is what makes you one person (same household).
  5. The display slot shows the chase: the Premium Dog Food Dynamic Product Ad featuring the exact product you viewed. If it doesn’t, reload once (you may have beaten the ~3s enroll). The Trace panel shows the display REQ then an IMP.
  6. Back on the shop → “Complete purchase” → a signed /v1/t/conv books a CPA conversion and suppresses you from the chase (you won’t be re-shown the thing you bought).
If the chase doesn’t appear, it’s almost always one of these:
  • Contextual-only consent on either tab → no identity → no match. Both must be “Accept personalized”.
  • Different persona on the two tabs (someone clicked “New visitor”/“Change”). Keep the same email on both.
  • Too fast — you opened/reloaded the publisher before the ~3s enroll. Wait, then reload.
  • You viewed only the shop home, not a product page (the product page is what fires the enroll pixel).
Verified live: the chase LI li-dogfood-cart-rt wins the publisher display auction with the DPA, matched via the shared demo household — a non-enrolled visitor gets a different ad.

These run in your host terminal (not here) — they need make demo-forward running. Watch them land in the Overview, the advertiser/publisher dashboards, or Grafana.
① Short, bounded load test — proves the money invariant live
make loadtest RPS=40 DURATION=2m VERIFY=1

~2 min at 40 req/s. VERIFY=1 asserts reporting counts match what was spent (zero slippage). Bump to RPS=80 DURATION=5m for a bigger fill; keep ≤110 on a laptop.

② Continuous trickle — leave running during the whole talk
make traffic DEMO_RPS=10

Steady background traffic (Ctrl-C to stop) so every dashboard stays live instead of frozen.

③ Audience-density run — draws users from the seeded synthetic universe
make loadtest RPS=50 DURATION=2m USER_POOL=2500 VERIFY=1

Hits real audience members so segment targeting + retargeting light up (the same 2,500-user pool the demo audiences are built from).

④ High-throughput fill & money run — the "under real pressure" demo
make loadtest RPS=150 DURATION=5m VERIFY=1

~5 min at 150 req/s — pushes the stack near its knee so fill, pacing, freq-cap re-auction and the money invariant all work under load. At 150+ rps VERIFY can read RED mid-run because it samples while the event spool is still draining — re-check the ClickHouse counts a few minutes after the run before calling loss. Keep ≤110 for latency work; 150–200 is for fill/money.

⑤ Re-light the wired-but-empty portal sections after a run
make demo-data

Repopulates invoices / conversions / webhooks / marketplace / partners and prunes stale rows.

Full protocol + baselines for a measured run (not a demo): the perf-loadtest skill. For a demo, ①–② are enough.
