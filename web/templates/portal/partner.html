{{ template "card-start" (dict "title" "Integration guide" "subtitle" "How to integrate with the exchange over OpenRTB") }}

DSP (demand): respond to our bid requests at your registered bid endpoint. Return a valid OpenRTB 2.5 BidResponse within your configured timeout (default 100ms). Include seatbid[].bid[].adm, price (CPM), and adomain. Honour the request's bcat/badv and floor.

SSP (supply): send us OpenRTB bid requests with a valid source.ext.schain and impression floors. See /sellers.json for seller identity.

  • Auth: send your sandbox API key as the X-API-Key header (generate one under API keys).
  • Sandbox runs real auctions with fake money — nothing you do bills.
  • When you're ready, your account manager runs the certification suite; passing promotes you to certified → active.

This guide is a summary; your account manager shares the full spec + a golden bid request during sandbox.
